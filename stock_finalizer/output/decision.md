# Final Investment Decision: NVIDIA

## Chosen Company
**NVIDIA**

## Why NVIDIA was selected
NVIDIA is the best company for investment among the three because it combines the strongest competitive moat, the highest profitability, and the clearest durable growth runway.

### Core reasons for selection
- **Best AI moat:** NVIDIA dominates AI accelerators and data center GPUs, supported by CUDA software, ecosystem lock-in, and broad developer adoption.
- **Highest margins:** Its gross margin is around 75%, materially above semiconductor peers, showing exceptional pricing power and business quality.
- **Most durable growth:** Demand is supported by hyperscaler capex, enterprise AI adoption, sovereign AI initiatives, and ongoing model scaling.
- **Best earnings quality:** NVIDIA’s revenue growth, profitability, and cash generation are all elite, making the business more resilient than the alternatives.
- **Clear leadership position:** It is the primary beneficiary of AI infrastructure spending and remains ahead in both product cadence and execution.

## Upside vs. risk

### Upside
- Continued AI infrastructure expansion should support strong revenue growth.
- New architectures like Blackwell and successors can sustain product leadership.
- Expansion into networking, CPUs, software, and full-stack AI platforms broadens the addressable market.
- The business can continue generating substantial free cash flow due to its margin profile and scale.

### Key risks
- **Valuation risk:** The stock trades at a premium, so multiple compression is possible if growth slows.
- **Customer concentration:** Dependence on a small number of hyperscalers creates concentration risk.
- **Geopolitical/export controls:** Restrictions involving China and other markets could reduce opportunity.
- **Competition:** Custom silicon and alternative accelerator architectures could pressure long-term share.
- **Execution risk:** Any product transition or software/platform adoption miss could weaken the growth story.

## Why the others were not selected

### Broadcom — not selected
Broadcom is a very strong company and the nearest competitor in quality, but it was excluded because:
- Its AI exposure is strong but **less pure** than NVIDIA’s.
- The business is **more diversified**, which reduces upside torque relative to NVIDIA’s concentrated AI leadership.
- It has excellent cash generation and software support, but the investment case is not as singular or as powerful as NVIDIA’s direct AI dominance.
- It remains a strong runner-up, especially for investors prioritizing diversification and cash flow, but it is not the top choice here.

### Amazon — not selected
Amazon is a high-quality long-term compounder, but it was excluded because:
- Its AI upside is **more indirect** than NVIDIA’s and Broadcom’s.
- Heavy capital spending, especially on AI infrastructure, suppresses near-term free cash flow.
- The company is exposed to multiple business lines, which makes the AI investment case less focused.
- While AWS and custom chips provide meaningful optionality, the investment thesis is broader and less concentrated than NVIDIA’s direct AI infrastructure leadership.

## Bottom line
**NVIDIA is the single best investment choice** because it offers the strongest combination of moat, margin, growth, and earnings durability. Broadcom is an excellent second choice, and Amazon is attractive as a diversified AI and cloud compounder, but neither matches NVIDIA’s superior AI leadership and risk-adjusted upside.
